= Valves
:description: One-way valves, flow limits, and what to do with valves built for vanilla.

A FicstFluids valve is a one-way ball valve with a small pressure loss.
Its *flow limit* set point is a hard cap, the only one in a network: pipes have none.

* On a liquid line the limit is in m³/min, and it can be set past a pipe's vanilla rating (300 m³/min on Mk1).
* On a gas line the limit is in std m³/min.

== Uses

* *Stopping a line.* Set the limit to 0.
* *One-way joins.* A valve keeps a return pump's pressure from pushing back up a supply line. See xref:liquid-networks.adoc#_returning_liquid_to_the_network[Returning liquid].
* *Charging a water tower* before opening it, then opening it part way. See xref:starting-up.adoc#_water_towers[Water towers].
* *Bringing a gas line up in sections.* See xref:gas-networks.adoc#_bring_a_big_line_up_in_sections[Gas networks].

== Valves built for vanilla

Valves built to work around vanilla's physics are probably no longer needed, and are fine left in.
Wide open, a valve costs very little: its pressure loss is about that of a few pipe widths of straight pipe.

CAUTION: Check its limit.
A valve throttled down to tame vanilla's sloshing, or to share flow between branches, still caps the flow at that setting.
It may now be what starves the machines past it.
Open it up, or take it out.
