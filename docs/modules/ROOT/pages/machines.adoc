= Machines
:description: How machine inlets draw and outlets push, and every machine's pressures.

== Inlets need pressure

Each machine inlet has a pressure band:

* below its *minimum* it draws nothing,
* at its *desired* pressure it draws as fast as it can,
* in between it draws part of that.

A Refinery's liquid inlet draws nothing at 4 m and draws fastest at 8 m.

== Full speed comes before the desired pressure

A machine draws to keep its input buffer topped up.
While the buffer refills it asks for up to *twice* its use rate; once full, just its use rate.
Generators take their water or fuel in loads, so they hold one load short of full.

Because of that doubling, a machine keeps up with its own use well below its desired pressure.
It runs at full speed from a quarter of the way up its band.

.A Refinery's liquid inlet
[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Inlet pressure | What happens

| below 4 m
| Draws nothing.

| 4 to 5 m
| Draws part of its rate; once its buffer is empty it slows (71% at 4.5 m).

| 5 to 8 m
| Runs at full speed; only its buffer refills more slowly.

| 8 m and up
| Runs at full speed and refills its buffer fastest.
|===

Gas inlets work the same way.
A Blender's nitrogen runs at full speed from about 5.9 MPa, its desired pressure being 6.9 MPa.

The xref:report.adoc[Network Status report] warns about an inlet below its full-speed pressure, says the speed the machine will drop to, and only notes one between full speed and desired.

== Machines change the network

This throttling is what makes a network settle rather than surge.
But a network running at its full flow has no spare: add a machine, and every inlet's pressure drops.
Machines below their full-speed pressure then draw only part of their rate.

== Outlets push at a fixed pressure

A machine's liquid outlet supplies at 10 m of head, plus its height.
Delivering higher than that, or through more friction, needs a pump.

== Machine pressures

All pressures are gauge. Liquid bands are the same for every liquid inlet: nothing below 4 m, desired 8 m, full speed from 5 m. Gas bands are written minimum–desired.
