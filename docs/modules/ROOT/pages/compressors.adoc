= Compressors
:description: A pipeline pump on a gas line: ratio limits, relief pressure, power, and stages.

A pipeline pump placed on a gas line works as a compressor.
The same building, Mk1 or Mk2, is a pump on a liquid line and a compressor on a gas one.

NOTE: Compressor numbers are still being tuned and may change.

== Ratio, not lift

A compressor multiplies the *absolute* pressure at its suction by up to its *ratio limit*.
The closer it runs to that limit, the less gas it moves; at the limit it moves none.

[cols="1,1,1"]
|===
| Compressor | Displacement | Ratio limit on nitrogen

| Mk1
| 300 m³/min at suction
| about 3.5

| Mk2
| 600 m³/min at suction
| about 7.9
|===

The ratio limit depends on the gas: on Dark Matter Residue a Mk2 reaches about 6.

Because the ratio multiplies absolute pressure, the suction pressure matters most.
A Mk2 taking nitrogen at 600 kPa gauge (about 700 kPa absolute) delivers up to about 5.5 MPa; at atmospheric suction, only about 0.7 MPa.

TIP: A compressor's displacement is measured at its suction.
Denser gas at the suction means more gas moved for the same displacement.

== Relief pressure

Every compressor has a relief valve set at about 82.7 MPa absolute (12,000 psi), above the highest machine inlet.
Above it, the relief valve returns part of the compressor's output to its suction, and about 10% above it the compressor moves nothing.

This is also what stops a gas freight platform or wagon from overfilling.

== Stages

Compressors in series multiply.
Two Mk2s reach about 7.9 × 7.9 times the first one's absolute suction pressure, less the line's losses between them.
The plasma chain needs at least two stages; see xref:gas-networks.adoc#_very_high_pressures_the_plasma_chain[Very high pressures].

== Power

A compressor draws power for the work it does.
Compressing hot gas costs far more: on Dark Matter Residue, about 4 MJ per kilogram at a ratio of 4.3.

== Draining a vessel

A compressor on an outlet can draw a vessel below atmospheric.
A Mk2 gets it to about a tenth of its discharge pressure; nearer vacuum takes compressors in series.
It never quite reaches empty, so to clear a gas platform completely, flush it.

== When the report warns

The xref:report.adoc[Network Status report] warns about a compressor at its ratio limit, a compressor relieving, and a powered compressor moving nothing.
