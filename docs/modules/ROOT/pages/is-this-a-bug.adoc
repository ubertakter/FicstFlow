= Is this a bug?
:description: Behavior that looks wrong but is FicstFluids working as designed.

All of these are correct behavior.
If what you see isn't here, or doesn't match the explanation, see xref:reporting-bugs.adoc[].

[cols="2,3"]
|===
| What you see | Why, and what to do

| My gas line stopped after installing FicstFluids.
| Vanilla gas lines have no compressors. Add one, then let the line fill. xref:gas-networks.adoc#_gas_in_an_existing_save[Gas in an existing save]

| The network won't come up to pressure at start-up.
| Empty pipes and refilling buffers ask for more than it can give. xref:starting-up.adoc[Start it in stages].

| A pump shows a negative lift and 0 kW.
| More flow is going through it than its runout. xref:pumps.adoc#_negative_lift[Negative lift]

| A pump with its power cut keeps turning.
| Flow from elsewhere is driving it like a turbine. Use a valve to stop a line. xref:pumps.adoc#_a_pump_with_its_power_cut_can_keep_turning[Pumps]

| Two pumps in series barely lift more than one.
| They each carry the whole flow. Put them in parallel. xref:pumps.adoc#_series_or_parallel[Series or parallel]

| A machine's water output backs up although the network wants water.
| Its outlet pushes only 10 m. Return the water with a pump. xref:liquid-networks.adoc#_returning_liquid_to_the_network[Returning liquid]

| One Refinery feeding another sits at about 5 m.
| An exactly matched chain. It still runs at full speed. xref:starting-up.adoc#_an_exactly_matched_chain[An exactly matched chain]

| A machine runs at full speed below its desired pressure.
| Full speed comes a quarter of the way up the band. xref:machines.adoc#_full_speed_comes_before_the_desired_pressure[Machines]

| The pipe fill percentages don't add up to the network's total.
| The fill is an estimate of how the pipe looks. xref:pipes.adoc#_the_fill_shown_on_the_panel[Pipes]

| A tank never drains below a certain level.
| A downhill outlet needs some depth over its mouth. xref:tanks.adoc[]

| A gas line delivers less than it takes in.
| The rest is packing into the pipe as pressure. xref:freight.adoc#_gas[Gas freight]

| A gas platform reads −101 kPa.
| An empty gas platform is at vacuum. xref:freight.adoc#_gas[Gas freight]

| A gas wagon or platform shows kilograms and a pressure, not m³.
| Gas is carried under pressure, so it is measured by mass. xref:freight.adoc#_gas[Gas freight]

| A loading platform won't push out of its outlet.
| Only one side works at a time. xref:freight.adoc#_only_one_side_works_at_a_time[Freight]

| A platform won't take a new fluid.
| Flush it first. xref:freight.adoc#_changing_a_platforms_fluid[Freight]

| A truck reads empty while docked.
| The transfer lands at the end of the 9 s dock. xref:freight.adoc#_only_one_side_works_at_a_time[Freight]

| An unloading station won't fill a Fluid Buffer.
| Its 10 m balances the buffer's level. Add a pump or lower the buffer. xref:freight.adoc#_it_empties_like_an_extractor[Freight]

| A pipe reads full, but the machine at its end has run out.
| Nothing is pushing the liquid: its supply has stopped, for example an unloading station that has run empty. A full pipe isn't a supply. xref:freight.adoc#_running_machines_from_an_unloading_station[Freight]

| The report warns about pumps on a line whose machines are on standby.
| They use power and move nothing. xref:report.adoc#_machines_on_standby_warn_about_their_pumps[Report]

| Placing a pump emptied the pipe.
| Placing a pump on a full pipe throws its contents away. xref:pipes.adoc#_placing_a_pump_on_a_full_pipe[Pipes]
|===
