= FicstFluids
:description: What FicstFluids does, and how this manual is laid out.

FicstFluids takes over Satisfactory's fluid physics.
Vanilla moves fluid between pipe segments by rules of its own: a pipe has a flow rating, a pump adds a fixed "head lift", and gas behaves like a liquid.
FicstFluids throws those rules out and solves the network the way an engineer would.

* *Pressure drives flow.* Fluid moves from high pressure to low, and every machine, pump and tank in a network pushes or pulls on the same pressures.
* *Pipes have friction, not a flow cap.* A short pipe can carry far more than a long one.
* *Pumps follow a pump curve.* The more you draw through a pump, the less it lifts.
* *Machines need pressure at their inlets* to draw at full rate, and push fluid out of their outlets at a set pressure.
* *Gas is compressible.* A pipeline pump on a gas line works as a compressor, gas lines store gas as pressure, and they take time to fill.
* *Freight platforms and truck stations* fill like tanks and empty like extractors, and carry gas under pressure.

FicstFluids also replaces the panels for pumps, pipes, valves, Fluid Buffers and compressors with ones that show its numbers.
It adds a *Network Status report*, a list of every network with what is wrong with it and where (see xref:report.adoc[]).

IMPORTANT: Networks built for vanilla will often not work under FicstFluids until you add pumps or compressors.
That is intended.
See xref:coming-from-vanilla.adoc[].

== How this manual is laid out

The *Guide* is meant to be read in order.
It starts with a xref:primer.adoc[fluid mechanics primer], because every other page uses its words: head, gauge pressure, friction, a pump curve.
Then it covers what changes from vanilla, how to design a network, how to start one up, and gas.

The *Reference* is meant to be looked up.
It has a page for each kind of building, the Network Status report, and xref:is-this-a-bug.adoc[], which lists the behaviour that looks wrong but isn't.

Found something that looks wrong?
Check xref:is-this-a-bug.adoc[] first, then see xref:reporting-bugs.adoc[].
